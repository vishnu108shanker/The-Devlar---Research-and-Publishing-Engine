The Devlar: system outline

A production side that writes, a website that reads, and Postgres as the only contract between them.

Devlar production layer (Python + Node workers)

Collectors, scrapers, deduplicatorsOfficial blogs, releases, advisories, papers. Reused from DevByte ideas.
Research enginePlanner, evidence gathering, claim verification. Python, LangGraph, RAG, knowledge graph.
Editorial layerTurns approved claims into an article in your reading style.
Quality gate and publishing engineMoves an article to published only if checks pass, and records why.

The Devlar website (PERN)

React front endTech news feed, article reader, source and claim inspection.
Express + Node APIAuth, CORS, helmet, rate limits. Reads article content, writes user data.
Keyword explanation featureSelect a term, press Alt+D, get Naive, Standard or Expert. Calls Groq through Express.
ChatGPT / Claude handoffButton that opens a chat with the article context and sources prefilled.
